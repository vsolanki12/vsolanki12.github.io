Executive Summary

  • A user-provided HostedCluster.spec.additionalTrustBundle propagated through the HostedControlPlane to the guest openshift-config/user-ca-bundle ConfigMap.
  • A source-only ConfigMap update propagated to the guest without changing the HostedControlPlane generation.
  • Removing the bundle removed the unused guest ConfigMap.
  • Pause and resume behavior was verified: reconciliation stopped while paused and resumed afterward.
  • A guest-side content change was repaired to match the source bundle.
  • A missing source generated the expected reconciliation error and recovered after source recreation.
  • The PR HCCO image started the user-ca-bundle controller and registered its watched resources.
  • Final result: the OCPBUGS-114723 user CA bundle reconciliation behavior passed the tested scenarios.
Data handling: kubeconfig contents, credentials, certificates, pull-secret data, and private endpoint details are excluded. The recorded hashes are SHA-256 values of test ca-bundle.crt data.
